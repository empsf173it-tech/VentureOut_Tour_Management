Our Story

Redefining How The World Travels

VentureOut was born out of a simple belief: travel should be more than just visiting places; it should be an enriching soul connection with cultures, landscapes, and wildlife.

Founded by veteran mountain explorer Alex Vance, our agency has grown from a local trekking outfit into an award-winning global operator connecting over 50,000 travelers to pristine locations in 70+ nations.

Our Purpose

Driven By Curiosity & Conservation

Every journey we design adheres to strict ethical standards. We empower local indigenous communities, fund local conservation projects, and preserve fragile ecosystems.

  • Community First: 80% of trip costs stay directly within destination communities.
  • Small Group Sizes: Intimate tour groups capped at 12 participants for personalized care.
  • Safety Protocol: Wilderness First Responder certified guides on all mountain expeditions.
  • Driven By Curiosity: We constantly seek out unexplored terrains and authentic experiences.
  • Conservation: Committed to protecting wildlife habitats and minimizing our ecological footprint.
